## SECTION 3: THE CORE PROBLEM THEY WANT SOLVED

Extracted from Q7 and Q8 — written in the client's language.

**Problem 1:** She doesn't know who she is anymore. After decades of living for her family, her work, and everyone else, she looks in the mirror and doesn't recognise the woman looking back. She's not just tired — she's invisible to herself.

**Problem 2:** Exhausted from balancing work and life with no time left for herself. The busy business owner is running the business, running the family, running everyone else's life — but nobody is running hers. She has no time and she doesn't feel she deserves to make any.

**Problem 3:** Suffering through menopause without support, understanding, or community. Her family criticises her mood swings. The world tells her to push through. She suffers in silence because nobody talks about it openly — and she feels alone in a journey that millions of women share.

**Problem 4:** Looking good on the outside while feeling empty on the inside. She maintains the appearance, shows up, performs — but the happiness is missing from within.

**Problem 5:** Loneliness and longing for a community of women who truly understand her. She sees other women who seem happy and successful and feels a quiet ache: "Why not me? Where did I go?"

**Why she hasn't solved it yet:**
- Regular holidays came and went — she returned the same, or more exhausted
- Weight loss programs and gym routines addressed the body but not the soul
- Scrolling Instagram gave temporary motivation with no lasting change
- She told everyone she was fine and just kept pushing through
- Self-help books sat highlighted on her shelf but nothing changed without community and accountability
- What was missing: a safe, soulful space with like-minded women who truly understand, led by someone who has walked the same path

## SECTION 4: WHAT THEY WANT INSTEAD

**Tangible outcome:** New daily practices — meditation, journaling, yoga, affirmations, better nutrition. Boundaries set. A community of like-minded women she can continue with after the retreat.

**Emotional outcome:** Reset, realigned, rejuvenated, heard, understood, connected, liberated, free, energized — and younger from the inside (not just looking younger, but genuinely feeling the energy and lightness of a younger self).

**Identity outcome:** A woman who chooses herself — without guilt. Who no longer waits for permission to exist for herself. Who sees herself as worthy of investment, time, and care. Who responds to life from conscious choice rather than past conditioning.

## SECTION 7: ICP PERSONA SUMMARY

She is between 42 and 58 years old, living in Dubai, UAE. She might be running her own business, managing a household, or balancing both with breathtaking competence that nobody outside her home ever questions. She has given the best years of her energy to her family, her career, and her community — and somewhere along the way, quietly and without announcement, she lost the thread back to herself. Menopause arrived without warning and without a support system, bringing mood swings her family didn't understand and hot flashes that arrived at the worst possible moments. She scrolls Instagram for inspiration, books regular holidays hoping something will shift, and tells everyone she's fine. She's not fine. She's exhausted, invisible to herself, and quietly longing for a space where women truly understand what she's carrying — and she's finally, tentatively, ready to choose herself.

- **Topics that resonate:** Menopause and midlife transitions told honestly · The invisible weight of carrying everyone else · Women's stories of transformation · The freedom of choosing yourself for the first time · Travel as soul work, not social media content · The five pillars of healing (Mind, Body, Energy, Emotion, Connection) · What sisterhood actually feels like for women 40+
