## SECTION 3: HOW DELIVERY WORKS

**Step 1 — Discovery & Onboarding:**
Interested woman fills out an intake form — designed to understand her specific situation: hormonal stage, emotional challenges, what she's seeking, what she's already tried.

**Step 2 — Booking & Pre-Retreat Communication:**
Confirmation of booking. Pre-retreat communication to build anticipation and set intentions.

**Step 3 — Arrival (Day 1 — Pause):**
Curated wellness welcome kit delivered on arrival (candle, crystal bracelet). Zoom check-in / orientation. Time to settle and find stillness. Evening: opening flow, introduction, intention setting, grounded meditation, conscious dining, breathwork, deep relaxation, journaling, silent reflection.

**Step 4 — The Retreat Days (Days 2–3 — Let Go & Reconnect):**
Consistent morning routine: sunrise meditation, gentle yoga, breathwork. Afternoon: explore the destination together. Evening programming alternates between: fire ceremony / candle release ritual (Day 2) and sharing circle / successful women's discussion (Day 3).

**Step 5 — Farewell (Day 4 — Return Renewed):**
Morning routine, nourishing breakfast, farewell ceremony. Women depart with new practices, a curated self-care plan, and an ongoing sisterhood.

**Step 6 — Post-Retreat:**
Community continues via WhatsApp or private group. Future studio gatherings for post-retreat integration.

## SECTION 9: COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

**Tone by context:**
- **LinkedIn posts:** Direct, warm, story-led. Lead with personal experience. Name menopause openly and without apology. Share transformation stories. Speak woman to woman, not coach to student.
- **Sales conversations:** Emotional before transactional. Lead with "Where are you right now?" not "Here's what you get." Listen first. The offer is the answer to what she shares.
- **Client onboarding:** Personal, intentional, and warm. The experience begins with the intake form and never feels administrative.

**Language to use:**
reset · realign · rise · soulful · meaningful · healing · sisterhood · reclaim · liberated · transformation · pause · heard · understood · freedom · your turn · soul · breathe · renewed · reconnect · chosen · journey · back to yourself

**Language to avoid:**
"empowerment" (as a hollow word) · "self-love" (overused) · "anti-aging" · "fix" or "cure" · "ladies" (too informal) · "amazing" (generic) · "program" (too corporate) · "hormonal issues" (clinical) · any language that implies she is broken

**Hard limits:**
- Never market She Travels as a sightseeing tour or social holiday
- Never suggest the outcome is looking younger — always feeling younger
- Never use language that makes women feel they need to have it together before they can come
- Never compare She Travels to competitors directly — own the category instead
