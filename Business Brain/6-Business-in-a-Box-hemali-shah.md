## SECTION 9: COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES
*(Practical application — not voice strategy)*

**Tone by context:**
- LinkedIn posts: Personal, story-driven, direct — speaking from lived experience rather than generic wellness messaging
- Sales conversations: Lead with the story and the specific release ritual, not a feature list of amenities
- Client onboarding: Warm but structured — clear about what the retreat actually involves (the 5 pillars, the journaling ritual) rather than vague promises

**Language to use:**
heard, holistic, release, reset, mind body energy emotion connection, meaningful travel, journaling, real connection, lived experience

**Language to avoid:**
generic self-care language, "find yourself," vague luxury-retreat marketing language that doesn't name the actual problem being solved

**Hard limits:**
Avoid letting content drift toward appearance-focused or competitive travel content (the exact dynamic Hemali is positioning against); avoid presenting this as wellness-only (massage/yoga) without the emotional release component front and center
