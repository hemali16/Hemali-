## THE TAGLINE

**"She Travels.Life is not where women find themselves.
It is where they choose themselves."**

---

## WHY THIS WORKS

**Finding** implies she was lost.
**Choosing** implies she has always had the power.

Finding is passive. It happens to you.
Choosing is active. It comes from you.

Finding suggests something was missing.
Choosing suggests the courage was always there — waiting.

She Travels.Life does not rescue women.
It gives them the space, the safety, and the sisterhood
to make the most courageous choice of their lives —
to choose themselves. Again. Fully. Without guilt.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS

**As the closing line of every post:**
She Travels.Life is not where women find themselves.
It is where they choose themselves.

**As the hero line of the one-pager:**
Replace "I Found Myself Again" with "I Chose Myself Again"
wherever it appears in any She Travels.Life material.

**As the brand positioning statement:**
When asked what She Travels.Life is — say this.
Not a travel company. Not a wellness retreat.
The place where women choose themselves.

**As the CTA reframe:**
Not "Come find yourself."
But "Come choose yourself."

**As the retreat promise:**
You will not leave saying — I found myself.
You will leave saying — I chose myself.
And that changes everything.

---

## THE DISTINCTION — USE IN CONTENT

|---------|----------|

| Someone else's map | Her own compass |
