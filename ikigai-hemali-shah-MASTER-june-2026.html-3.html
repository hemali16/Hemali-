SECTION 14
What to Stop Doing (Subtractive Clarity)
  1. Stop managing big corporate travel groups. They are misaligned with your mission, expensive to deliver, and emotionally empty. Every hour spent there is an hour stolen from She Travels Life.
  2. Stop marketing City One as your primary identity. Rebrand yourself as a transformation leader. City One is the vehicle. She Travels Life is the destination.
  3. Stop justifying low pricing to compete. Big players will always undercut you. Your value is in transformation and ethics — not budget logistics. Charge premium or lose your soul.
  4. Stop doing all the logistics yourself. Hire. Delegate. Automate. You are a transformation person, not a logistics person. Protect that distinction fiercely.
  5. Stop hiding your story. Your vulnerability and resilience are your biggest competitive advantage. Lead with them everywhere. Always. The woman who survived is more credible than the woman with the perfect Instagram.
  6. Stop waiting for permission. From your husband, from your family, from the market, from timing. You've already proven you can. The evidence is 14 years and 10 Tripadvisor Excellence awards. You don't need permission anymore. Stand in that.
