Her Inner World

Her Pain Points
The 5 Pains She Carries
  • Invisible at home: Her voice is undermined. Her opinions are dismissed. Her family takes her for granted. She is the support system for everyone — but who supports her?
  • Fragmented wellness: She spends separately on salons, spas, yoga, coaches — but nothing connects. There is no one trusted platform. She doesn't know whom to rely on.
  • No community of her own: She has social circles but no women who truly *get her* — women at the same life stage, with the same questions, on the same journey of reclaiming themselves.
  • Travel without agency: She has always traveled for others — for the family, for the husband's preference. The idea of traveling *for herself*, on her terms, feels selfish. Or scary. Or both.
  • The age myth: Society tells her that 50+ is too late to reinvent. She half-believes it. She needs proof — real, human proof — that it's possible. (That's you, Hemali.)

Your 3-Tier Product Line
🌸 TIER 1 · The Circle (Entry Experience)
What: A half-day community gathering — sound healing, women's sharing circle, guided meditation, light refreshments. Led by Hemali + 1 guest expert (healer, coach, or speaker).
Where: Your yoga studio at home, a rented community space, or rooftop venue.
Who: 10-20 women. Open to all. No travel required.
Price: AED 200–400 per person
Purpose: Community building, lead generation for retreats, proof of demand.
Frequency: Monthly
✈️ TIER 2 · The Retreat (Core Experience) — LAUNCH SEPTEMBER
What: 3-day holistic retreat. All 5 pillars: sunrise meditation + yoga flow + breath work (morning) · wellness lunch + rest + integration (afternoon) · fire ceremony + candle release + gratitude under open sky (night).
Where: Coastal, mountain, or nature location — UAE, Oman, India
Who: 8–15 women. Intimate, intentional, curated.
What's included: Accommodation, all meals, all sessions, 1 expert guest, She Travel journal + welcome gift
Price: AED 2,500–3,500 per person
Revenue at 10 women: AED 25,000–35,000
Frequency: Quarterly

What: 5-7 day curated luxury retreat to an international destination. Experiential travel: beachfront, mountain sanctuary, desert retreat, or cultural immersion. Fully curated by Hemali. Small group (4-6 women max).

Frequency: 1-2 times per year
