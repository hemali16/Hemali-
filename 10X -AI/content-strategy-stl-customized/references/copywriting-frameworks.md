# Reference File for Content Strategy Skill

## STORY-FIRST FRAMEWORK (Core for Hemali)

This is the default structure for 60% of posts (especially Pillars 1, 2, 4).

### The Arc
1. **Specific moment** (age, location, who was there)
2. **What I faced** (internal struggle, not external obstacle)
3. **What I realized** (the turning point)
4. **What changed** (the outcome)
5. **What it means for you** (bridge to Reena)

### Example Template:
"At 50, I was standing alone in Madrid for the first time in my life. And I was terrified. I'd never made a decision just for me. I'd always done what was expected.

Walking those streets at night, I realized something: I was choosing fear instead of myself. Every time.

So I kept walking. Alone. No itinerary. No safety plan.

And on day 3, something shifted. I wasn't the same woman who arrived.

This is what Reena is seeking. Not a vacation. A rebirth."

### Why it works:
- Specific details (age, Madrid, alone, night)
- Vulnerability (terrified, never made a choice for self)
- Turning point (realized I was choosing fear)
- Action (kept walking)
- Transformation (wasn't the same)
- Bridge to Reena (names her, shows relevance)

## TEACHING FRAMEWORK (For Pillar 4, 5)

When Hemali is teaching/reframing, use this structure:

### The Arc
1. **What most people believe** (the old story, from Reena's POV)
2. **Why that belief is incomplete** (what's missing)
3. **What's actually true** (the reframe)
4. **Why it changes everything** (implications)
5. **What to do with this** (permission, not instruction)

### Example Template:
"Most people believe: 'Midlife is a downhill journey. It's all decline after 40.'

But here's what's missing from that story: At 40+, you finally know yourself. You've survived heartbreak, failure, sacrifice. You've earned wisdom.

What's actually true: Midlife is a launching pad. You're not declining. You're distilling.

Why it changes everything: You stop fighting your age and start leveraging your experience.

So here's my permission for you: Stop apologizing for being 40+. Start claiming the power that only comes with time."

### Why it works:
- Names the old belief (Reena recognizes it)
- Shows the gap (acknowledges why she believed it)
- States the reframe (clear, non-preachy)
- Shows implications (makes it relevant)
- Gives permission (not instruction, not sales pitch)

## PROOF FRAMEWORK (For Pillar 3, testimonial posts)

When sharing proof (business results, mentorship outcomes, organic demand):

### The Arc
1. **Who they were** (before, in their context)
2. **What was stuck** (their specific pain/limitation)
3. **What changed** (what was different in the process)
4. **Who they became** (after, measurable or felt)
5. **Why it matters** (what it proves about She Travels.Life)

### Example Template:
"Kalpana came to me 5 years ago. She was brilliant but dependent. No income of her own. No confidence in her skills.

She was stuck in the belief: 'I'm a homemaker. That's my role.'

Then we worked together. Not on confidence talks. On creating value. On small steps. Consistently.

Today, Kalpana earns 15,000 rupees per month from her own waxing and henna business. She stands on her own feet. She's proud of herself.

This is what She Travels.Life does. It doesn't tell you you're worthy. It shows you how to create evidence of your own worth."

### Why it works:
- Names the person (not "a client," not generic)
- Shows the context (married, homemaker, dependent)
- Names the exact belief (specific to her pain)
- Shows the process (not just outcome)
- Gives measurable outcome (15,000 rupees, specific)
- Ties back to STL (shows what the company promises)

## TESTIMONIAL REFRAMING FRAMEWORK (Pillar 3, client voices)

When publishing a client's words, use this structure to frame it:

### The Arc
1. **Why I'm sharing this** (vulnerability about what it means)
2. **Who said it** (name, context)
3. **What they shared** (their exact words, not paraphrase)
4. **What it reveals** (what it proves about the work)
5. **What it's an invitation for** (call to action for others)

### Example Template:
"This message made me cry.

After the 4-day retreat, a woman wrote:

'I came invisible. I left visible to myself. For the first time in 30 years, I know who I am outside of what I do for others.'

This is not a testimonial. This is a woman claiming her power.

And it proves something I've always known: The transformation doesn't happen because I'm special. It happens because she finally chose herself in a space that held her while she did.

If you've ever felt invisible, this is your permission slip. The space exists. The ritual works. And you're worth the pause."

### Why it works:
- Hemali's vulnerability (this made me cry)
- Names the person (builds trust)
- Uses exact words (not paraphrased)
- Interprets the meaning (not left to chance)
- Shows what it proves (about the work, not about her)
- Invitation, not hard sell (permission slip language)

## CONTROVERSY/REFRAME FRAMEWORK (Pillars 1, 4, 5)

When Hemali is going against the grain (challenging Instagram travel, generic wellness, etc.):

### The Arc
1. **What everyone says** (the conventional wisdom)
2. **Why it sounds right** (the emotional appeal)
3. **But here's what I've seen** (Hemali's lived experience)
4. **So here's what's actually true** (the reframe)
5. **What to do differently** (permission + action)

### Example Template:
"Instagram travel says: 'Collect experiences. Document moments. Share your life.'

It sounds right. It sounds like living.

But here's what I've seen: Women come home with perfect photos and the same old pain. They performed for the camera instead of showing up for themselves.

So here's what's actually true: Real transformation requires you to be unseen. Unperformed. Unapologetic.

Travel as a portal for inner work is not Instagram-worthy. It's soul-worthy.

So here's my invitation: Stop collecting photos. Start collecting wisdom."

### Why it works:
- Acknowledges why the old way appeals (not dismissive)
- Grounds counter-claim in lived experience (not theory)
- Reframes the value (soul > shares)
- Gives permission to be different (unperformed)
- Invites a different path (not attacking the old one)

## QUESTION FRAMEWORK (For engagement)

When Hemali wants deep engagement, use strategic questions:

### The Arc
1. **Context question** (sets up why this matters)
2. **Personal question** (makes it about Reena)
3. **Revealing question** (gets at the pain)
4. **Permission question** (opens possibility)

### Example Template:
"How many times have you chosen what was expected over what you wanted?

And when you did choose yourself, how did it feel?

Do you remember the last time someone asked you 'What do YOU need?' instead of 'How can I help?'

What if midlife was the moment you finally got permission to choose yourself in everything?"

### Why it works:
- Context question (acknowledges her pattern)
- Personal question (makes it about her experience)
- Revealing question (gets at the pain and the memory of choosing self)
- Permission question (opens the door)
- End on Reena's possibility (not Hemali's offer)

## ANNOUNCEMENT FRAMEWORK (Pillar 5, for retreat launches)

When announcing retreats or new offerings:

### The Arc
1. **Why I'm excited** (Hemali's authentic emotion)
2. **What it is** (clear, specific)
3. **Who it's for** (Reena, not everyone)
4. **Why now** (urgency without pressure)
5. **How to join** (warm invitation)

### Example Template:
"I'm so excited to share this.

The September 4-day retreat is open for registration. 8 women. 4 days of silence, story-sharing, the 5-pillar journey, the release ritual, and community that sees you fully.

This is for you if you've felt invisible at home and you're ready to choose yourself.

We're starting September 10th because the women I've spoken to said: 'I need to do this before winter.' And I believe them.

If you're ready, here's how to apply: [link]

This retreat will change you. Not because I'm special. But because you finally chose yourself."

### Why it works:
- Authentic emotion (excited, proud)
- Specific details (8 women, 4 days, specific practices)
- Named for Reena (invisible at home, ready to choose self)
- Urgency tied to real reason (not artificial pressure)
- Warm invitation (not aggressive sales copy)
- Proof at the end (you chose yourself, not Hemali's magic)

## COMMUNITY/BELONGING FRAMEWORK (Pillar 3)

When building belonging and connection:

### The Arc
1. **What isolation feels like** (in Reena's words)
2. **What she's craving** (specific, not vague)
3. **What community actually is** (not surface bonding)
4. **Why it changes things** (healing happens here)
5. **What's being created** (the invitation)

### Example Template:
"You feel invisible. Unheard. Like nobody really listens to you the way you need.

What you're craving is to be seen. Fully. Without performing.

And community isn't 50 women at a spa pretending to be friends. It's 6–8 women in a circle telling the truth. Holding space. Witnessing transformation.

Healing happens here. Not because the space is fancy. But because you're finally not alone in your struggle.

She Travels.Life Monthly Community is that circle. Starting next month. 1,500 AED. One evening. One circle. Your people."

### Why it works:
- Names her pain (isolation, invisibility)
- Names what she's craving (specific, not generic)
- Reframes community (not surface, but deep)
- Shows why it works (healing through witness)
- Invites her in (not selling features, selling belonging)

## FRAMEWORK SELECTION GUIDE

**Use Story-First Framework when:**
- Sharing Hemali's personal journey (Madrid, yoga, 25 years)
- Opening a new topic
- Building authority through lived experience
- You want emotional resonance (60% of posts)

**Use Teaching Framework when:**
- Reframing beliefs (midlife, wellness, pain)
- Sharing wisdom hard-won through experience
- Pillar 4 (Wisdom Within) content
- You want clarity + permission

**Use Proof Framework when:**
- Sharing business results (14 years, Kalpana, employees)
- Building credibility for She Travels.Life
- Pillar 3 (Healing in Community) testimonials
- You want evidence that it works

**Use Question Framework when:**
- You want engagement (not just reach)
- Making content feel intimate (Reena answering for herself)
- Deep connection > viral reach
- 1–2 questions per post

**Use Announcement Framework when:**
- Launching retreats
- Opening registration
- Pillar 5 (Reset-Realign-Rise) calls to action
- Moving toward conversion

**Use Controversy/Reframe Framework when:**
- Contrasting with competitors
- Going against industry grain
- Positioning She Travels.Life as different
- Pillar 2 (Travel as Portal) content

**Use Testimonial Reframing Framework when:**
- Sharing client/community member experiences
- Building social proof
- Showing transformation
- Pillar 3 (Healing in Community) content

## FRAMEWORK ROTATION (90 Days)

Don't use the same framework twice in a row.

**Week 1:** Story-First (Pillar 1)
**Week 2:** Teaching (Pillar 4)
**Week 3:** Story-First (Pillar 2)
**Week 4:** Question (Engagement)
**Week 5:** Proof (Kalpana/mentorship)
**Week 6:** Announcement (Retreat)

This creates rhythm and variety. Reena never gets bored. Hemali never sounds repetitive.

---

Remember: **The framework is not the content. The content is Hemali's voice living through the framework.**

Never let the structure show. Let only the humanity and the wisdom come through.

The framework is scaffolding. Hemali is the art.
