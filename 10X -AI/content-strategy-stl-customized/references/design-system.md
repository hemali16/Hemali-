## BRAND COLORS (Twilight/Sunrise Palette)

She Travels.Life uses a warm, evocative color palette inspired by twilight and sunrise — never dark luxury.

### Primary Colors

**Coral-Pink** (#E8967F)
- Primary accent color
- Used for headlines, key phrases, CTAs
- Warm, inviting, grounded
- Associated with dawn, rebirth, feminine power

**Golden** (#D4A574)
- Secondary accent color
- Used for supporting elements, secondary headlines
- Warmth, wisdom, inner light
- Associated with transformed energy

**Dusky Lavender** (#9B8BA8)
- Tertiary accent color
- Used for subtle highlights, borders, backgrounds
- Introspection, transition, sacredness
- Associated with twilight, inner work

### Neutral Colors

**Warm Cream** (#F5F3EF)
- Background color
- Off-white, not pure white
- Soft, welcoming, readable

**Charcoal** (#2D2D2D)
- Text color (not pure black)
- Warm dark gray
- Readable, not harsh

**Light Gray** (#D9D6D0)
- Dividers, secondary text, subtle emphasis
- Soft contrast, not stark

## TYPOGRAPHY

### Headlines (Primary Typeface)

**Font:** Playfair Display OR Lora (serif, classic, dignified)
- Size: 32–48px for main headlines
- Size: 24–32px for subheads
- Weight: Bold (700) for contrast, Regular (400) for elegance
- All caps: Never (unless for accent)
- Line height: 1.3 (tight for visual impact)
- Example: "Midlife Isn't a Pause. It's a Launching Pad."

### Body Text (Secondary Typeface)

**Font:** Inter OR Open Sans (sans-serif, modern, readable)
- Size: 16–18px for main body copy
- Size: 14–16px for secondary copy
- Weight: Regular (400) for default, Medium (500) for emphasis
- Line height: 1.6 (loose for readability)
- All caps: Never (use bold instead)
- Example: "She Travels.Life serves women 40+ navigating life's transitions..."

### Accent Text (Tertiary)

**Font:** Italic Serif (Playfair Display Italic or Lora Italic)
- Used for Hemali's signature phrases
- Color: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Size: Match body size or slightly larger
- Example: *"Nothing is impossible in this world."*

## VISUAL HIERARCHY

### Level 1 — Main Headline
- Size: 36–48px
- Color: Charcoal (#2D2D2D)
- Font: Playfair Display Bold
- Margin below: 20px
- Purpose: Primary positioning, core claim

### Level 2 — Subheading
- Size: 24–28px
- Color: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Font: Playfair Display Regular
- Margin below: 16px
- Purpose: Supporting claim, bridge to body

### Level 3 — Body Copy
- Size: 16–18px
- Color: Charcoal (#2D2D2D)
- Font: Inter Regular
- Line height: 1.6
- Margin below: 12–16px
- Purpose: Main narrative, storytelling

### Level 4 — Accent/Signature
- Size: 14–16px
- Color: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Font: Lora Italic or Playfair Display Bold
- Margin: 8px above/below
- Purpose: Hemali's voice, signature phrases

### Level 5 — Secondary/Supporting
- Size: 14px
- Color: Light Gray (#D9D6D0)
- Font: Inter Regular
- Purpose: Testimonials, details, supporting info

---

## SPACING & LAYOUT

### White Space (Sacred)
- Minimum margin around blocks: 20px
- Minimum padding within blocks: 20px
- Never feel cramped. White space = breathing room.

### Column Width
- Max width for body text: 600px (optimal reading width)
- Wider layouts feel scattered. Narrower feels cramped.

### Line Breaks
- After headlines: 20px
- After paragraphs: 16px
- Between sections: 24–32px
- Creates rhythm, prevents visual fatigue

---

## VISUAL ELEMENTS

### Dividers
- Style: Simple horizontal line
- Color: Light Gray (#D9D6D0) or Dusky Lavender (#9B8BA8)
- Height: 1–2px
- Used to: Separate sections without harsh breaks

### Accent Underlines
- Color: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Style: 3–4px solid
- Used under: Key phrases, Hemali's signature, section headers

### Borders (Subtle)
- Color: Dusky Lavender (#9B8BA8)
- Width: 1–2px
- Opacity: 60–80% (never harsh)
- Used for: Testimonial blocks, quote blocks, key callouts

### Background Blocks
- Color: Dusky Lavender (#9B8BA8) at 10–15% opacity
- Never solid color (too harsh)
- Always semi-transparent
- Used for: Key messaging, testimonials, CTAs

## IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

### Aesthetic (NOT Dark Luxury)
- Warm, golden-hour lighting (sunrise/sunset)
- Real people, not stock perfection
- Vulnerable moments (tears, quiet reflection, raw emotion)
- Community in circles (not posed group shots)
- Travel as backdrop (not hero)
- Hemali and participants visible (not hidden behind aesthetics)

### Color Grading
- Warm filter (amber, golden tones)
- Never cool blue tones
- Saturation: Rich but not over-saturated
- Contrast: Soft, not harsh
- Matches brand palette (coral, gold, lavender tones in photos)

### What to Avoid (Dark Luxury Traps)
- Minimalist white spaces
- Cool gray/blue palettes
- Posed, perfect beauty shots
- Slick, hyper-professional photography
- Stock photo feeling
- Dark, moody aesthetics
- Absence of people or emotion

### Photography Subject Matter
- Real retreats (candids from August staycation, September retreat)
- Hemali's journey (Madrid photos, yoga, personal moments)
- Community moments (women in circles, vulnerable faces, holding)
- Travel as vessel (but always secondary to inner transformation)
- Hemali's wisdom (her face, her presence, her story)

## ICON & SYMBOL USAGE

### Primary Symbols (Minimal)
- Compass (🧭) — journey, direction, inner navigation
- Sunrise (🌅) — rebirth, rising, dawn of new chapter
- Fire/Spark (✨) — transformation, awakening, power
- Water (waves) — flow, healing, release

### These appear in:
- LinkedIn post headers (1 symbol, max)
- Email subject lines (1 symbol, max)
- Website sections (sparingly)

### Never use:
- Multiple symbols in one post (creates noise)
- Decorative emoji (flowers, hearts, etc. — unless intentional)
- Generic wellness symbols (chakras, mandalas without context)

## BUTTON & CTA STYLING

### Primary CTA Button
- Background: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Text: Warm Cream (#F5F3EF), bold, 16px
- Padding: 12–16px horizontal, 10–12px vertical
- Border radius: 4–6px (subtle rounding)
- Hover state: Golden (#D4A574) background
- Text: "Join the Waitlist" or "Explore the Retreat"

### Secondary CTA (Text Link)
- Color: Coral-Pink (#E8967F)
- Underline: None (underline on hover)
- Font: Inter Medium, 16px
- Text: "Learn More" or "Tell Me More"

### Tertiary CTA (Subtle)
- Color: Dusky Lavender (#9B8BA8)
- Font: Inter Regular, 14px
- Text: "Not sure yet? Read stories." or "Browse all retreats"

### CTA Messaging (Never Sales-y)
- "Join the circle"
- "Explore what's possible"
- "Claim your space"
- "Let's talk about your journey"
- "Apply to the retreat"
- AVOID: "Buy now," "Limited offer," "Act fast," "Secure your spot"

## ACCESSIBILITY

### Color Contrast
- Text (Charcoal) on Warm Cream: ✅ 9:1 contrast (WCAG AAA)
- Text (Charcoal) on Dusky Lavender: ✅ 7:1 contrast (WCAG AA)
- Buttons (Cream on Coral): ✅ 8:1 contrast (WCAG AA)

### Font Sizing
- Never smaller than 14px body text
- Never smaller than 12px secondary text
- Ensures readability on all devices

### Line Height
- Body: 1.6 minimum (readability)
- Headlines: 1.3 (visual impact)
- Never cramped

---

## MOBILE OPTIMIZATION

### Responsive Design
- Main headline: 28px on mobile, 36–48px on desktop
- Subheading: 18px on mobile, 24–28px on desktop
- Body copy: 16px (consistent across devices)
- Spacing: Maintains 16px minimum padding on mobile

### Stack Order
- Single column on mobile (not cramped side-by-side)
- Images scale to full width
- CTAs full-width on mobile
