## SENTENCE RHYTHM

**Hemali's rhythm is distinctive. Match it.**

**Pattern:** Long narrative build (25–40 words) → short payoff (5–10 words)

Example:
- Long: "I spent 25 years adapting to what everyone else needed, at home, at work, in my family, until one day I woke up and didn't recognize the woman in the mirror."
- Short: "That's when everything changed."

Never open with the short line. The story EARNS the payoff.

## VOICE RULES (DO's & DON'Ts)

**✅ DO:**
- Open with specific personal moment (age, location, who was there)
- Name exact numbers, dates, ages (12 rupees, 75 paise, age 57, 25 years married)
- Use short sentences after long stories (rhythm contrast)
- Include at least one signature phrase per post
- Speak like you're talking to a friend (conversational, not formal)
- Lead with story, extract lesson second
- Name specific people when they appear (Hemali, Reena, Kalpana, Sangeeta)
- Be vulnerable about fear before the victory (fear → action → outcome)
- Use Reena's pain language (her exact words, not paraphrases)

**❌ DON'T:**
- Start with the lesson/headline, then story (reverse it)
- Use corporate jargon or wellness clichés
- Say "I'm passionate about..." or "You deserve..."
- Paraphrase Reena's pain (use her exact words)
- Use bullet points in social posts (one flowing paragraph or short broken lines)
- Write about "finding yourself" (say "rediscovering" or "reclaiming")
- Use the phrase "self-care" without context (specify what you mean)
- Sound like a LinkedIn coach (generic frameworks, no specificity)
- Make claims without proof (always ground in Hemali's story, 14-year business, Kalpana, etc.)
- Hide the voice (let Hemali ring through every word)

## CONTENT TONE BY CONTEXT

**Adjust slightly based on post type, but always stay warm and wise.**

### Story posts (Pillar 1, 2, 4)
Tone: Vulnerable, intimate, narrative. Like sharing with a trusted friend.
Example: "At 50, I found myself standing alone in Madrid for the first time in my life. And I was terrified..."

### Teaching posts (Pillar 4, 5)
Tone: Wise, direct, grounded. Like a mentor sharing hard-won wisdom.
Example: "Here's what I learned: the pain you've been carrying isn't yours. It's just what you inherited. And you get to put it down."

### Testimonial posts (Pillar 3)
Tone: Warm, proud, witnessed. Like introducing a sister who just transformed.
Example: "This is what the first woman to walk through She Travels.Life's doors told me after the retreat..."

### Announcement posts (Pillar 5)
Tone: Inviting, clear, action-oriented. Like opening a door and saying "come through."
Example: "The September retreat is open. And I want you to know exactly what you're signing up for..."
