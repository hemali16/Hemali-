## THE INVISIBILITY DIAGNOSTIC (4/4 Test)

Every post must score 4/4 on these dimensions:

**1. VOICE — Is this unmistakably Hemali?**
Generic LinkedIn language = 0. Signature phrases, story rhythm, vulnerability = 1.

**2. POV — Lived Experience?**
Theory/frameworks = 0. Specific moment, her age, her story = 1.

**3. SPECIFICITY — Does Reena recognize herself?**
Generic "women 40+" = 0. Reena's exact pain language = 1.

**4. ASKING — Does it invite engagement?**
Hard sell or no CTA = 0. Warm question Reena wants to answer = 1.

If < 4/4, rewrite before posting.

## THE 9-SCRUB SYSTEM

Run these on every completed post before publishing.

### SCRUB 1 — EM DASH REMOVAL
Remove every em dash (—). Every single one.
Never replace with hyphen. Rewrite the sentence.

| Use case | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pause mid-sentence | Split into two sentences |
| Connecting clauses | Full stop. New sentence. |
| Adding emphasis | Remove. End on strong word. |
| Introducing explanation | Use colon or rewrite |

**Before:** "The skill works — but only if your Voice DNA is specific."
**After:** "The skill works. But only if your Voice DNA is specific."

- You deserve it

**Instead:** End on a warm question, an invitation, or Hemali's voice ringing through.

### SCRUB 5 — SENTENCE STRUCTURE & RHYTHM

**Hemali's rhythm:**
- Long narrative (25–40 words) → short payoff (5–10 words)
- Never reverse it (lesson first, then story)
- Vary sentence length (short, medium, long, short)
- Use fragments sparingly (only for emphasis)

**Check:**
- Do sentences feel conversational (not formal)?
- Is there rhythm contrast (varied length)?
- Does the story come before the payoff?
- Would Hemali say this aloud exactly as written?

If any no — rewrite for rhythm.

---

### SCRUB 6 — TONAL & LOGIC PATTERNS

**AI patterns to remove:**

**Filler phrases:**
"Let's be real" · "If I'm being honest" · "Look, here's the thing" · "The truth is" (repeated) · "Not gonna lie" · "Real talk" (every post)

**False transitions:**
"And here's why that matters" (said after every story beat)
"So here's what I did" (forced setup)
"But wait, there's more" (fake suspense)

**Manufactured tension:**
Opening with a question to "hook" when the story itself is the hook.
Creating artificial conflict where none exists.
Overstating stakes ("this will change your life" when talking about a small insight).

**One-way broadcasts:**
Stories with no engagement CTA.
Teaching with no room for Reena's voice.
Announcements with no invitation.

**Check:**
- Does this feel authentic or constructed?
- Is there tension or is it forced?
- Can Reena engage, or is it one-way broadcast?
- Would Hemali feel proud reading this aloud?

If no to any — rewrite.

### SCRUB 7 — SPECIFICITY & CREDIBILITY

Every claim must be grounded in specificity.

**Generic → Specific:**

| Generic | Specific |
|---|---|
| "I was unhappy" | "I spent 25 years adapting to everyone else's needs" |
| "I healed" | "Yoga healed my knee instead of surgery" |
| "Success is possible" | "At 57, I have more energy than I did at 37" |
| "Transformation happens" | "After 3 days in silence with 6 other women, something shifted" |
| "We help women" | "Kalpana went from 0 income to 15,000 rupees/month in 4 months" |

**Credibility check:**
- Is every claim tied to proof (story, business, mentorship, demand)?
- Are numbers exact (not "many," "some," "many")? (25 years, 3,500 AED, 14 years, 30 people, 4.8 rating)
- Are people named (Hemali, Reena, Kalpana, Sangeeta) not generic ("a client," "women," "friends")?
- Does Reena have reason to believe this works?

If not — add specificity before posting.

## STRUCTURAL RULES

### Post Structure (Sacred Order)
1. **Open:** Specific moment (age, location, who was there)
2. **Build:** Long narrative (25–40 words) showing struggle/discovery
3. **Payoff:** Short sentence (5–10 words) stating the insight
4. **Bridge:** Connect to Reena (her pain or her potential)
5. **Invite:** Warm question or invitation (not hard sell)

**Never reverse this order. Story earns the lesson, not the other way around.**

### Paragraph Length
- Short: 1–3 sentences (creates emphasis)
- Medium: 4–6 sentences (default rhythm)
- Long: 7+ sentences (only for narrative build)
- Never write a paragraph longer than 8 sentences

### Line Breaks
Use line breaks to:
- Create rhythm contrast
- Emphasize a short sentence
- Give Reena white space to breathe

**Not for:** Turning prose into bullet points (banned).

## ENGAGEMENT STANDARDS

### CTA (Call To Action)

**Warm CTAs (Hemali's style):**
- "Where are you in this journey?"
- "What resonates for you?"
- "What's one thing you're tired of carrying?"
- "Tell me: who told you that you were too old?"
- "What would reset feel like for you?"

**Hard sell CTAs (BANNED):**
- "DM me NOW"
- "Click the link"
- "Book your spot"
- "Limited spaces"
- "Don't wait"
- "Act now"

Hemali invites. She doesn't push.

### Engagement Invitation
Every post must have a question that Reena wants to answer.
Not: "Do you agree?" (too generic)
Yes: "When did you stop choosing yourself?" (specific to her pain)
